## IDRBT (opened https://www.idrbt.ac.in/about-idrbt -> About-IDRBT.pdf, 2026-09-29)
- Established March 1996 by RBI as a Society under the Societies Registration Act. Financially independent since 2004.
- Governing Council chaired by N. S. Vishwanathan (ex-DG RBI), non-executive chairman.
- INFINET launched June 1999 (closed user group network; now MPLS). SFMS launched 14 Dec 2001. SFMS used by NEFT, RTGS, DvP for G-secs, CFMS.
- IDRBT initiated NFS Aug 2003, launched Aug 2004; handed NFS operations to NPCI Oct 2009. NFS as of 31 Jul 2024: 2.61 lakh ATMs of 1,317 member banks.
- IBCC unveiled 2 Aug 2013. Feb 2015: INFINET, SFMS, RTGS, NEFT, IBCC systems hived off to subsidiary IFTAS; IFTAS transferred to RBI in 2019.
- RTGS launched 26 Mar 2004; NEFT Nov 2005; both developed at IDRBT on SFMS + INFINET. NEFT 24x7 since Dec 2019; RTGS 24x7 since Dec 2020.
- Certifying Authority for BFSI under IT Act 2000 (since 2002).
- KNOWN ERROR #18 "deemed university": NOT SUPPORTED. Source describes a registered society. Verdict: false as stated (no deemed-university status in official description).
- KNOWN ERROR #8 "NFS 1.2 million ATMs": FALSE. 2.61 lakh ATMs as of 31 Jul 2024 (IDRBT). RuPay 60% of debit cards: still unverified.

## DICGC (opened https://www.dicgc.org.in/guide-to-deposit-insurance, 2026-09-29)
- Insured limit Rs 5 lakh per depositor per bank (principal + interest), same capacity/right aggregated. VERIFIED primary.
- Limit raised from Rs 1 lakh w.e.f. 4 Feb 2020 (BS/Capital Market report of DICGC statement) - reported.
- Premium: flat 12 paise per Rs 100 assessable deposits historically; Risk-Based Premium framework issued 6 Feb 2026, effective 1 Apr 2026; flat rate is ceiling and still applies to LABs, payments banks, UCBs (ICRA research; BS; CENFA). Reported; DICGC circular S1803/04-01-046/2025-2026 not opened.

- Wholly owned subsidiary of RBI (Business Standard 2025, multiple). Needs a primary line (DICGC about page).
