## What it does

The Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation protects depositors if a bank fails. <Fact id="dicgc-limit" show="claim" /> <Fact id="dicgc-wos" show="claim" /> <Fact id="dicgc-mumbai" show="claim" />

## How it works with banks

Insured banks pay DICGC a premium on their assessable deposits. <Fact id="dicgc-rbp-2026" show="claim" /> For a bank's technology teams, deposit insurance matters mainly through data: a bank must be able to produce accurate, depositor-level records quickly, so that claims can be paid if it is ever needed.
