## What it does

The Clearing Corporation of India stands between the two sides of trades in government securities, money markets, foreign exchange and rupee derivatives, so that each side's risk is on CCIL rather than on the other party. <Fact id="ccil-authorised" show="claim" />

## Why banks care

Banks' treasury systems send trades to CCIL for clearing and settlement, and its rules for margins and settlement timing shape how those systems are built.
