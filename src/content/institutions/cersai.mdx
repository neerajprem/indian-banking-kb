## What it does

CERSAI keeps two national registries that banks rely on. The first records security interests, so a lender can check whether a property already secures another loan. <Fact id="cersai-govt-2011" show="claim" /> The second holds central KYC records. <Fact id="ckycr-cersai" show="claim" />

## A Government company

Earlier drafts of this site said RBI had promoted it. RBI's own circular describes it as a Government company. It appears on this page because banks and other lenders regulated by RBI use its registries every day.
