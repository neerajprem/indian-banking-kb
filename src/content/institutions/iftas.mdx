## What it does

Indian Financial Technology and Allied Services runs the network and messaging layers that RBI's payment systems depend on. <Fact id="iftas-wos" show="claim" />

## Where its systems came from

IFTAS took over systems originally built at IDRBT. <Fact id="iftas-hiveoff-2015" show="claim" /> <Fact id="infinet-1999" show="claim" /> <Fact id="sfms-2001" show="claim" />

## Why banks care

Every bank that sends RTGS or NEFT payments connects through this infrastructure, so its availability matters to the whole system. Smaller banks may also use its shared platform: <Fact id="ibcc-cbs" show="claim" />
