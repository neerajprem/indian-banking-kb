## What it does

Bharatiya Reserve Bank Note Mudran prints banknotes for RBI. <Fact id="brbnmpl-1995" show="claim" /> <Fact id="brbnmpl-capacity" show="claim" />

## Why it belongs here

BRBNMPL is part of the currency side of RBI rather than digital payments, and it is included because it is one of RBI's wholly owned subsidiaries. Its connection to banking technology is indirect: cash still moves through branches, ATMs and currency chests that banks run.
