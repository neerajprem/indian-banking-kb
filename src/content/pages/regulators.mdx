## Who regulates what

A bank's technology answers to more than one authority. RBI regulates banks and payment systems; other bodies cover cyber incidents, identity, personal data and the financial markets in which banks also operate.

## How rules are made

RBI usually consults before it regulates. It publishes a draft, invites comments, then issues a final instrument. The 2025 authentication framework followed that path: <Fact id="auth-drafts" show="claim" /> <Fact id="auth-2025" show="claim" /> Model risk guidance is at the draft stage now: <Fact id="mrm-draft-2026" show="claim" />

RBI's rules take a few forms:
- **Master Directions** set out all the rules on a subject in one place and are updated as the rules change.
- **Circulars** issue specific instructions.
- **Master Circulars** consolidate circulars on a subject as of a date.

Other bodies use their own instruments: CERT-In issues directions under the IT Act, and MeitY notifies rules under the laws it administers.

## How rules are enforced

RBI supervises banks through off-site returns and on-site inspections, including IT examinations. When it finds weaknesses, it issues corrective action plans; when those are not met, it can restrict business.

- **Payment data storage.** <Fact id="amex-diners-2021" show="claim" /> <Fact id="mastercard-restriction-2021" show="claim" /> <Fact id="mastercard-lifted-2022" show="claim" />
- **IT resilience.** <Fact id="kotak-2024" show="claim" />

The pattern is consistent. Restrictions target growth, such as new customers or new cards, rather than existing customers, and they are lifted once the regulator is satisfied the gaps are closed. [Limitations and known problems](/limitations/) describes these cases in more detail.

## Questions for a compliance review

- Which of the mandates above apply to this system, and who owns evidence for each?
- Where is this system's payment data stored, including backups and logs?
- Could a cyber incident here be reported to CERT-In and RBI within 6 hours?
- Which parts of this system are outsourced, and is the outsourcing assessment complete?
- Which rules affecting this system are still in draft, and who is tracking them?
