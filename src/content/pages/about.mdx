## Why this guide exists

The technology behind Indian banking is documented in pieces: regulators' circulars, operators' specifications, vendors' brochures and the occasional press report. Each piece is accurate about its own corner and silent about the rest. Engineers joining the industry, and many who have worked in it for years, end up assembling the whole picture from memory and hallway conversations.

This guide puts that picture in one place. It starts with how a payment moves, opens up the core banking system, and works outwards through architecture, technology choices, scale, regulation and the institutions around the Reserve Bank.

## How it is kept honest

Every number, date, limit and named regulation on this site comes from a register of facts, each tied to a source that was opened and read, and each carrying the date it was true. Claims that could not be confirmed are left out rather than guessed, and claims that turned out to be wrong are recorded as corrections. The details are on the [sources and methodology](/sources/) page.
