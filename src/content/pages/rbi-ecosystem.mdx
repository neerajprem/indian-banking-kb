## Three kinds of relationship with RBI

Earlier descriptions often called all of these bodies "RBI subsidiaries". The sources show three different relationships:

- **Wholly owned by RBI.** DICGC, BRBNMPL, ReBIT, IFTAS and RBIH are companies that RBI owns outright. <Fact id="dicgc-wos" show="claim" />
- **Set up by RBI or at its instance, but not owned by it.** IDRBT is a society RBI established. NPCI and CCIL were created at RBI's initiative but are owned by banks and financial institutions. <Fact id="npci-2008" show="claim" />
- **Owned by the Government of India.** NABARD and NHB were once held by RBI, which sold its stakes by 2019, and CERSAI is a Government company. <Fact id="nabard-nhb-divest-2019" show="claim" /> <Fact id="cersai-govt-2011" show="claim" />
