## How to read this section

Each topic starts with a landscape table of the main options and what they are good at, then explains how to choose, the trade-offs and the common pitfalls, and ends with questions for a design review. Product names appear as plain text for reference only; the site does not recommend vendors. Indian users of a product are named only where a public source confirms it.
